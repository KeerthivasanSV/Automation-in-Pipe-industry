# **Project Plan for Robotic Arm Simulation and Optimization**  
**Deadline:** February 28th  

---  

## **Step 1: Simulation (Jan 25 - Feb 6)**  

We are planning to break down the simulation into three parts:  

1. **Straw Picking (Jan 25 - Jan 30):**  
   - The robotic arm should be able to pick up the straw smoothly.  
   - We'll focus on getting the grip right and ensuring the arm follows an accurate trajectory.  
   - Testing different grasping strategies to make sure the straw is held securely will be a key focus.  

2. **Holding and Moving (Jan 31 - Feb 3):**  
   - After picking up the straw, the arm should move slightly to the right and hold it for 10 seconds.  
   - We'll work on fine-tuning the movement and checking for any stability issues.  

3. **Precision Placement (Feb 4 - Feb 6):**  
   - This part will involve implementing the precision placement algorithm to ensure the straw is placed exactly onto the target circle.  
   - We'll focus on accuracy and incorporate feedback mechanisms if needed.  

---

## **Step 2: Algorithm Development (Feb 7 - Feb 21)**  

1. **Image Processing – Contour Edge Detection (Feb 7 - Feb 10):**  
   - We'll develop an algorithm to detect contours and edges using derivatives.  
   - The goal is to make detection efficient and accurate to assist in precise placement.  

2. **Force Optimization (Feb 11 - Feb 14):**  
   - The robotic arm needs to apply just the right amount of force to pick up the straw without crushing or dropping it.  
   - We'll implement and fine-tune a force optimization algorithm to handle this.  

3. **Precision Placement Timing (Feb 15 - Feb 21):**  
   - The arm should place the straw at two specific locations, one after the other, with a time delay in between.  
   - Our focus will be on getting the timing and position accuracy perfect.  

---

## **Step 3: Testing and Refinement (Feb 22 - Feb 26)**  

- Once everything is put together, we'll conduct full-scale simulations to ensure all components are functioning as expected.  
- We'll identify and resolve any bugs or inefficiencies.  
- Performance tuning and consistency checks will be a major part of this phase.  

---

## **Step 4: Final Touches (Feb 27 - Feb 28)**  

- We'll prepare all necessary documentation, reports, and presentations.  
- Final tests will be conducted to ensure everything is working smoothly before submission.  
- The goal is to wrap things up without any last-minute stress.
